What do mean, median and mode tell us here?

The mean adds up every year's change and divides by the number of years. The median is the middle value once the years are in order, so one very good or very bad year moves it less. The mode is the value that comes up most often; most regions never repeat a figure, so they have no mode. "Overall" chains the yearly changes together to show how far prices moved in total.
